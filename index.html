About

I'm an urban analyst and PhD researcher. I work on how transportation, housing, neighborhoods, and regional economies change, and what that means for equity and resilience, using econometrics, machine learning, and spatial analysis. I care about work that reaches beyond the journal: public-facing, policy-oriented, and collaborative across disciplines.

My published work spans transportation, regional economic development and resilience, and equity, appearing in journals including Economic Development Quarterly, Sustainability, and Computers, Environment and Urban Systems.

Every project here moves from raw data to a clear, defensible recommendation a planning agency, research center, or consultancy could act on.
