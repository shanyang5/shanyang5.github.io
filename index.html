About

I'm a researcher in city and regional planning with a focus on spatial methods — spatial econometrics, accessibility modeling, and machine-learning approaches with interpretable outputs (XGBoost / SHAP).

My published work spans transportation safety and equity, transit accessibility, and regional economic resilience, appearing in journals including Regional Studies, Economic Development Quarterly, Sustainability, and Computers, Environment and Urban Systems.

I care about analysis that ends in a decision. Every project here moves from open data to a clear, defensible recommendation a planning agency or consultancy could act on.
